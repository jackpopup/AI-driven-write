# Direction Brief — Vibe Coding & the Software Industry

## Phase 1: Plan Output

### Agent Proposals Summary

**Senior Journalist** proposed a data-driven, WSJ-style feature anchored in market numbers (GitHub Copilot's 2M+ subscribers, Cursor's $9B valuation, Y Combinator batch data) with honest risk assessment. Strongest on sourcing and credibility.

**Copywriter** proposed a provocative, conversational piece with an irresistible hook (the cost of building software dropped 10x) and a practical "what to do Monday morning" payoff. Strongest on engagement and call-to-action.

**Producer** proposed a narrative-led piece opening with a character's first experience with vibe coding, set against the historical arc of democratization. Strongest on emotional resonance and memorability.

**Editor** proposed a clear, scannable structure optimized for busy business readers — subheads, concrete examples, segmented advice by company stage. Strongest on audience fit and information architecture.

---

### Synthesized Direction

**Top 3 Title Options:**
1. "Beyond the Hype: How Vibe Coding Is Quietly Reshaping the Software Industry"
2. "You Don't Need a CTO to Build Software Anymore — Here's What That Means for Your Business"
3. "From Prompt to Product: The Vibe Coding Shift Every Business Leader Needs to Understand"

**Core Message (1 sentence):**
Vibe coding — building software by describing what you want in natural language — has crossed from novelty to business reality, and the leaders who understand it now will have a decisive advantage in the next 3-5 years.

**Target Audience:**
Non-technical founders and business leaders (C-suite, VPs, board members) who keep hearing "vibe coding" but need a grounded, actionable understanding.

**Tone Direction:**
Authoritative but conversational. Accessible without being condescending. Real examples, honest about limitations. Think: smart business friend who happens to understand tech deeply.

**Proposed Structure:**
1. **Opening Hook** (~150 words) — A concrete, jaw-dropping example that makes the shift tangible
2. **What Vibe Coding Actually Is** (~200 words) — Plain-language explanation, Karpathy origin, how it works in practice
3. **The Evidence It's Real** (~300 words) — Market data, company examples, Y Combinator stats, Cursor/Copilot/Replit
4. **What This Means for Your Business** (~400 words) — Three strategic implications: speed to market, talent strategy, competitive dynamics
5. **When It Works and When It Doesn't** (~200 words) — Honest limitations, security concerns, technical debt, when you still need engineers
6. **What to Do Now** (~200 words) — Actionable steps for different company stages
7. **The Bigger Picture** (~100 words) — Forward-looking close about domain expertise becoming the real competitive moat

**Estimated Length:** ~1,500 words
**Estimated Read Time:** ~7 minutes
